The unit of work

A lot is one tracking number, broken into individual SKUs — nothing sells whole. Three kinds arrive: manifested and matching, manifested with a variance (short, over, or both), and blind — no manifest at all. The intake screen has a tab for each so you can see how differently they behave.

Cost basis is earned, not given

A manifested lot spreads its cost across expected units. A short lot re-spreads that cost over fewer units and files a claim. Over-shipped units enter at zero cost. A blind lot divides what you paid by whatever you have counted so far — so its cost per unit is provisional and falls as counting continues.

One number rules

Because you buy at a % of MRP, recovery — revenue earned as a share of what you paid — is the spine of the dashboard and the analytics page. Revenue and orders today sit on top; units received vs. listed sits right beside them.
