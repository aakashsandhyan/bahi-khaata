Three surfaces for the cart-continuity change, in the Palletworks system the dashboard already wears. Every figure below is realistic shop data. The panels are clickable — tap the pills, rows, and bills.

3 · The day, end to end

How the pieces behave

1Refresh-proof: the screen remembers its cart and re-opens it after any reload — a cart only dies by payment, by Clear, or by the next-morning sweep.

2Hold → serve → return: Meera forgets her wallet in the car — Hold cart, ring the next customer, tap Carts → her row → Resume. Her lines and her name come back exactly.

3Cross-register: a cart held on Register 2 resumes on Register 1 — the queue moves, the cart follows the customer.

4Morning sweep: yesterday's untouched carts are abandoned automatically at first touch of the day — the list always shows today's real work only.
